About Lamborghini

Lamborghini was founded in 1963 in Italy by Ferruccio Lamborghini. The brand is famous for its bold styling, aggressive aerodynamics, and roaring engines. Lamborghini cars are built for speed, luxury, and pure excitement.

Founded 1963
Founder Ferruccio Lamborghini
Country Italy
Specialty V10 & V12 Supercars
